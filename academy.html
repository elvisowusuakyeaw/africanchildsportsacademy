Our Categories of Development

We operate three categories of training programs according to the age groups

group

BASIC ( U - 13)

U13 who are mainly those between the ages of 9 to 12years are selected from the basic school games .This soccer program is designed to promote skill development, teamwork, sportsmanship, and a love for the game. It typically includes age-appropriate training sessions focusing on fundamental techniques such as passing, dribbling, shooting, and defensive skills. The program emphasizes fun, fair play, and learning the rules of soccer, while also encouraging physical fitness and social interaction. It features friendly matches, tournaments, and coaching by experienced trainers who understand the developmental needs of young players. Overall, the goal is to nurture young talents, build confidence, and foster a lifelong passion for soccer.

group

YOUTH (13 - 15years)

Our Under-15 Boys' Youth Soccer Program is designed to foster a love for the game while developing essential skills, teamwork, and sportsmanship. Open to boys aged 13 to 15, this program provides a structured environment for players to improve their technical abilities, tactical understanding, and physical fitness. Through weekly training sessions and competitive matches, participants will learn the fundamentals of dribbling, passing, shooting, and defensive techniques. Our experienced coaches emphasize positive attitude, discipline, and fair play both on and off the field.

group

PRE-PROFESIONAL (up to 18years)

Our pre-professional soccer program for boys aged 16-18 years is a specialized training and development initiative designed to prepare talented young athletes for professional or semi-professional soccer careers. The program typically combines advanced technical and tactical training, physical conditioning, and mental skills development. It provides exposure to competitive matches, scouting opportunities, and mentorship from experienced coaches and former players. The goal is to refine players' skills, enhance their understanding of the game, and foster professionalism, teamwork, and discipline to help them transition successfully into higher levels of competitive soccer. A highly accomplished and results-driven coach with more than two decades of expertise in empowering individuals and organizations to unlock their full potential. Adept at designing and implementing coaching programs that foster leadership development, enhance team performance, and drive organizational growth. Extensive background in talent management, including talent acquisition, succession planning, and employee development strategies.

Empowering young footballers through excellence, education and opportunity.
