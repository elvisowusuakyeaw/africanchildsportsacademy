Welcome To African Child Sports Academy

Where African Talents Meet Global Opportunities

African Child Sports Academy is committed to identifying, nurturing and developing talented young footballers while providing quality education, character development and professional opportunities.

Our main goal is to prepare players to compete at the highest level of football while becoming highly educated in their respective field of study.

Learn More Our Academy
Who We Are

Empowering dreams through Football

We believe every child deserves the opportunity to develop their football talent while receiving quality education.

Our Mission

To identify, develop, and elevate talented young footballers across Africa, providing them with the skills, discipline, and opportunities needed to succeed on the global stage.

Our Vision

A future where African children are recognized as some of the best footballers in the world, inspiring generations and promoting sportsmanship, teamwork, and leadership.
